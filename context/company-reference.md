## Consequences for our model

- The product mix is boilers, heat pumps and tanks: mostly **assembly** plus component production (heat exchangers). The group draft's "60% battery production" does not fit and should be revised.
- The heat pump market grew fast and then dropped after 2023. Extra capacity is only worth money when it can be sold: this is the main sensitivity in the business case.
- Better use cases than generic predictive maintenance may be closer to the product: leak testing, end-of-line testing of heat pumps, brazing and welding quality.
