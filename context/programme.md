## Group assignment rules (kick-off, module 1)

- Work as a group on an "area of concern" case; use the lecturers' presentations and literature on Howspace as much as possible.
- Write the PoV as a **paper of 6–8 pages** and a **presentation of max. 10 slides**, following the PoV guidelines on Howspace.
- Each group presents in **15 minutes** on the final day; a professional jury assesses the case work and the presentation.

## Paper structure (from the writing guides in `materials/writing-guides/`)

Position-paper outline, hourglass model:

1. **Introduction**: hook (the pilot works but does not scale), context, thesis, roadmap of the paper.
2. **Counter-arguments and rebuttal**: for example "roll the pilot out everywhere now" and "let every plant choose for itself".
3. **Argument 1 – value**: business case and investment gates.
4. **Argument 2 – design**: architecture, Business Activity Model, risks and COBIT.
5. **Argument 3 – organisation**: governance, SAFe, talent, change.
6. **Conclusion**: restate the thesis, the five decisions, plan of action.
7. **References** in APA style.

Each body paragraph: topic sentence, evidence, analysis, transition.
