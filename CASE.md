# CASE.md: Group 3, Case Digital Transformation (Buderus)

Source: `materials/case/case-description-group-3.jpg` (Advanced IT no. 15, group assignment NJ 2026, case 3). Summary in our own words.

## Situation

- Buderus is a multinational in the **energy sector** with several production and assembly sites.
- The energy transition and growth increased demand, and with it the pressure on production efficiency, quality and operating costs.
- The landscape mixes legacy applications, industrial systems, sensors and local OT solutions.
- A **successful pilot** at one site used sensor data to monitor installations and products online. It shows potential for predictive maintenance, higher availability and better quality.
- OT systems are business-critical, not all equipment is easy to integrate, and there is reluctance towards changes that could affect production continuity.

## The question

> How do you optimise IT/OT governance so that data analytics, AI and IoT are structurally integrated into production and maintenance, with enough grip on safety, continuity and scalability?

**Core tension:** how do you standardise digitally at group level without ignoring plant-specific OT constraints?

Management wants to integrate business and IT(IV) chains further, with **SAFe** as the operating model for change. Top priority is a **scalable OT/IoT/data/AI architecture** that lets several plants realise use cases within central standards.
