# Frameworks to apply

| Framework | What we use it for | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Business Activity Model** (Wijers / Anderson MacGyver, multimodality) | Decide per activity what is group standard and what stays plant-specific: Common, Adaptive, Specialized, Distinct | Workpage §8 |
| **Business Model & AI Canvas** | Per use case: for whom value, which process changes, which data and models, which human decision is supported, which risks and costs | Workpage §6 |
| **COBIT 2019** | Governance and accountability: who decides, who owns, how we measure. Map each risk to governance/management objectives (e.g. APO13, DSS05, BAI06) | Workpage §11 |
| **IEC 62443** | OT security: zones and conduits, security levels | Workpage §11 |
| **ISO/IEC 27001** | Information security management system | Workpage §11 |
| **ISO/IEC 42001** | AI management system: risk, transparency, human oversight | Workpage §11 |
| **ISA-95 / Purdue model** | Layering of the architecture (L0–L4 and the L3.5 OT-DMZ) | Workpage §6a, §7 |
| **SAFe** | Delivery model required by the case: product teams per use case, platform teams for shared capabilities, one ART "Digital Operations" | Workpage §10 |
| **Gartner Top Strategic Technology Trends 2026** | Top-3 only, motivated: Physical AI, AI Security Platforms, Domain-Specific Language Models (alternative: Preemptive Cybersecurity) | Workpage §12 |

## Business Activity Model in one table

| | Stable | Responsive |
|---|---|---|
| **Specific** | Specialized: niche solutions, in-house expertise, run/change ~60/40 | Distinct: bespoke, innovation, long-term partner, run/change ~20/80 |
| **Generic** | Common: interchangeable solutions, cost and hierarchy, run/change ~80/20 | Adaptive: proven market solutions, scalable, run/change ~40/60 |

Classify the activity (the "what"), not the system. The modality drives technology, sourcing, data, leadership and control choices.
